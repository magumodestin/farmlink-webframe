Step 03 — Features
What the UI needs to support
💡
Smart pricing
Shows farmers a suggested price range based on nearby listings and seasonal demand.
Phase 2
📶
Offline / low data mode
Core listings cached locally. Works on 2G. Images compressed automatically.
MVP
🔥
Waste alert discounts
Farmers mark stock as urgent — auto badge with % discount visible to nearby buyers.
MVP
⭐
Ratings & verification
Both farmers and buyers are rated. New farmers get verified badge after first 3 sales.
Phase 2
🏢
Bulk buying (B2B)
Restaurants and shops can post bulk buy requests. Farmers respond with quotes.
Phase 3
💬
WhatsApp integration
MVP flow — buyer taps WhatsApp button to contact farmer directly. No payment system needed yet.
